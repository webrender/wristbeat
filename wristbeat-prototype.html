How it maps to Wear OS

  • Audio clockSong position comes from AudioContext.getOutputTimestamp(). On the watch that's AAudioStream_getTimestamp via Oboe.
  • Input timeTaps are judged by event.timeStamp, not by frame. On the watch: MotionEvent.getEventTime().
  • HapticsThe bezel pulse stands in for a Vibrator tick on each cue.
  • ContentEach stage is a chart: BPM plus a list of cue beats. Music, sound effects and sprites are all code.
